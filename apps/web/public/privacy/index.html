Policy

Privacy Policy

Last updated October 26, 2026

ChewMash is designed to keep your Dining Dollars information on your device. You do not need a ChewMash cloud account to use the app.

What ChewMash uses

When you choose to sync or import dining data, ChewMash uses structured transaction details such as date, time, dining location, purchase amount, and an available Dining Dollars balance to build your dashboard. Picks may also request public Cal Poly menu information.

What ChewMash does not intentionally collect

ChewMash does not intentionally collect or store your Cal Poly username or password, authentication cookies, session tokens, student ID or card number, or raw GET page HTML.

Storage and sharing

Dining history and plan settings are stored locally on your device or in your browser. ChewMash does not sell dining data, use dining history for advertising, or send your private Dining Dollars transaction history to Dine On Campus.

Your controls

You can clear imported dining data from ChewMash, export a local backup, or reset the app. Clearing browser or app data may remove your local copy.

Questions or changes

ChewMash is an independent project. Policy changes will be published on this page. For technical questions, visit the IT Support page.
