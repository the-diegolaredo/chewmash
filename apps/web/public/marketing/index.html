Marketing Policy

Last updated October 26, 2026

ChewMash may promote product features, updates, and availability through its website, app-store listings, or public social channels.

No advertising use of dining data

ChewMash does not use your Dining Dollars transaction history, balance, Cal Poly credentials, or student identifiers for targeted advertising or marketing, and does not sell that information to advertisers.

Direct marketing

ChewMash does not currently require marketing email or text-message enrollment to use the product. If optional direct marketing communications are introduced later, they will require a clear opt-in and will include a way to stop those messages.

Product notices

Important service, privacy, security, or product notices are not treated as advertising and may be displayed inside ChewMash when needed.
